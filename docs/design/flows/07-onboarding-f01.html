Self-review — design bar & spec checklist (flow 07)

Design bar
  1. Provenance on every derived number, skipped-fields-estimated — current weight (measured · Health Connect / user-entered), target (user-entered), pace→kg conversion (derived), forecast (ESTIMATED until ≥ 14 days of data, R-A5), budget (formula estimate until F07 measures), skipped schedule (estimated). Nothing renders naked. ✓
  2. Tone — “later”, never “incomplete” — every skip is a “Later →”, the wall copy negotiates instead of obeying, safety reads as respect (“the fastest we’ll suggest”), zero guilt/shame strings; banned-list audit clean (no failed/missed/over/incomplete). ✓
  3. No red, no alarm — the strongest color anywhere is amber on the wall marker; refusals are haptic + copy, never hue. Ranges and skips render neutral/accent. ✓
  4. Zero-account / offline — the architecture sentence is the second sentence of the product; airplane-mode chip on screen 1; no email field, no sign-up, no spinner in any of the 8 steps; the only cloud touchpoint (refinement) is v1.x and consented at point of use (F12). ✓
  5. First-win design — forecast on screen ~70 s in, ≤ 30 s after goal entry, honestly hedged, screenshot-survives; the value is delivered before any ask beyond a goal. ✓
  6. Density — welcome is the only airy screen (deliberate: one decision); every other screen is receipt-dense; whitespace spent at the dials and the bloom. ✓
  7. R-U15 — n/a (no capture assists exist in the wizard); its spirit is honored structurally: the import branch is an equal path, free text is the last resort, and manual always lands somewhere. ✓
  8. R-U7 naming — quiz chips are foods and “exclude / okay”, never verdicts; no taboo vocabulary anywhere. ✓
