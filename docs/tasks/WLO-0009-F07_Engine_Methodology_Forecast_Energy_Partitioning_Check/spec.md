## Context

Two F07 §10 open questions that shape the flagship's published math and
ritual design. Both are owner calls because F07's trust model is "every
constant and assumption published on the Algorithms page" — what we assume
is part of the product.

## Q1 — Forecast energy partitioning

Should the 3-band forecast model body composition when projecting
bodyweight change (fat-loss fraction informed by F05 training context —
preserved lean mass means ~7,700 kcal/kg overstates late-cut cost), or
stay partition-free with the single published constant (R-A1)?

- Partition-free v1 (recommended): one constant, one honest caveat line;
  composition-aware rates ship [v1.x] with their own published assumptions.
- Fat-fraction from day one: more honest near goal, but the deceleration
  story gains a second layer of assumptions to defend.

## Q2 — Check-in cadence

Weekly is frozen as the ritual default (R-U2). Do power users get an
opt-in daily cadence in v1, or does daily wait to protect the Sunday
ritual's identity?

- Weekly-only v1 (recommended): daily unlock is [v1.x], informed by how
  users actually behave with the weekly card.
- Daily opt-in v1: satisfies the high-frequency minority immediately.

1. Both decisions recorded as R-* rulings in FEATURES.md §3.
2. F07 §3/§8 updated to match; §6 items removed if tracked there.
